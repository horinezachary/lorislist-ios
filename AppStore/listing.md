## Description (4000 max)
Lori's List keeps every shopping trip organized and in sync with the people you shop with.

Make a list for each store or occasion (groceries, the hardware store, a holiday dinner) and give each one its own color so you can spot it at a glance. Add items with an optional quantity, tap to cross them off as they go in the cart, and clear the crossed-off ones when you're done.

SHARE WITH YOUR HOUSEHOLD
• Invite anyone by email to a list.
• Choose whether they can edit it or only view it.
• Changes show up on everyone's phone right away.

SHOP ANYWHERE
• Your lists are saved on your phone, so they open instantly, even with no signal.
• Changes you make offline are sent as soon as you're back online.

QUICK TO USE
• Suggestions from things you've bought before, so common items take one tap.
• Rows drawn in each list's own color.
• Works on iPhone and iPad, and with your account on lorislist.horinetech.com.

STAY IN CONTROL
• See every device you're signed in on and sign out a lost phone remotely.
• Change your name, email or password from the app.
